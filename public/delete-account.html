AquaRythu Account & Data Deletion

Google Play Account Deletion Policy Compliance

AquaRythu empowers users to permanently delete their account and all associated personal and farm operational data at any time.

Method 1: Direct In-App Deletion (Instant)

1. Open the AquaRythu app.
2. Open the Profile tab.
3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
4. Confirm deletion. Your profile, farms, ponds, and feeding records are removed immediately.

Method 2: Request Deletion via Web Form / Support

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot log in, email our support team directly from your registered email or phone:

Email: support@aquarythu.com
Subject: Account Deletion Request
Information to Include: Registered Phone Number and/or Email Address.

What Data is Deleted?

  • User profile: Name, phone number, email, and authentication credentials.
  • Farms and pond configurations: Farm locations, ponds, stocking history.
  • Operational logs: Daily feeding logs, tray checks, water tests, sampling, and harvest records.
  • FCM push notification device tokens.

What Data is Retained?

In accordance with statutory tax and financial reporting laws, anonymized records of completed financial orders or invoices may be retained for accounting compliance purposes only. Records you created on a farm owned by someone else (for example as a team member) stay with that farm but are no longer linked to your name or account.
